How to download a course’s transcripts

  1. 1

    Pin the icon to your toolbar

    Open your browser’s Extensions menu (the puzzle-piece icon in the top-right toolbar) and pin Udemy Transcript Downloader so the button and its live progress badge stay visible.

  2. 2

    Open a Udemy course lecture

    Open the course player of any course you’re enrolled in — not the course’s landing page. If you already had a Udemy tab open before installing, reload that tab once.

    Open Udemy My learning
  3. 3

    Click the toolbar icon and start

    Click the Udemy Transcript Downloader icon in your toolbar, pick a caption language if needed, and click Start Download. Keep that course tab open until the ZIP finishes downloading.

Good to know

  • Live toolbar badge: while downloading, the icon shows the tab’s percentage (grey while paused, ✓ when finished, ! on error).
  • Saved courses: completed and paused courses appear under Saved courses in the popup from any tab, so you can save a ZIP again without re-downloading.
  • Local only: transcripts are fetched directly from Udemy using your active session and stored only in your browser. You can reopen this guide anytime via the ? button in the popup.
